About MIF-10

MIF-10 was developed using specific biomarker assays. Please use the units shown above.

Assay information

GDF-15 was measured using the R&D Systems Quantikine assay (DGD150), and PRO-C3 and PRO-C6 using Nordic Bioscience assays. Measurements obtained from other assay platforms may not be directly interchangeable and may require additional validation or recalibration.

Model equation
Z = −47.18 + 0.948·ln(GDF15+1) + 0.525·ln(YKL40+1) + 0.732·ln(PROC3+1) + 1.409·ln(PROC6+1) − 2.500·ln(LDL+1) + 1.247·ln(TG+1) + 0.417·ln(HbA1c+1) + 21.300·ln(INR+1) + 4.335·ln(TP+1) + 0.204·ln(ALT+1); P = 1 / (1 + e−Z)

Pre-specified thresholds: Rule-out < 0.284; Grey zone 0.284 to < 0.403; Rule-in ≥ 0.403.

MIF-10 results should be interpreted in the appropriate clinical and research context.
